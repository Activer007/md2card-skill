# 分页，不改写 / Pagination without rewriting

PARA_A01: 中文内容与 English notes should remain recognizably ours. A page boundary is a reading aid, not permission to shorten a sentence or invent a conclusion.

PARA_A02: Start with a clear observation. Our fictional reading group collects short field notes, keeps the original wording, and gives every observation a stable place in the sequence.

PARA_A03: 一个段落表达一个完整想法。即使下一张卡片仍有空间，也不应该把同一个段落的前半句和后半句分开。Keep the original punctuation: “yes”, ‘no’, and a useful ampersand &.

## 先保留结构 / Preserve structure first
### 连续标题 / Connected headings

PARA_A04: A heading and the paragraph that explains it belong together. When there is no room for both, move the complete group to the next card rather than leaving a title alone.

PARA_A05: Formatting carries meaning. Keep **important words**, *a quiet emphasis*, and `literal_code` exactly as the author supplied them; do not replace these details with a generic summary.

PARA_A06: 阅读顺序也属于内容。A reader should encounter the setup before the observation, the observation before the explanation, and the explanation before the final invitation to think.

## 再检查边界 / Check the boundaries

PARA_A07: The [field note reference](https://example.org/field-notes?lang=en&view=full "Original field-note destination") is an illustrative link. Pagination must preserve its label, destination, query string, and title.

PARA_A08: 版面留白不是错误。A short final card can be the honest consequence of keeping paragraphs whole. We prefer a clean boundary to a dense page that cuts the reader's thought in half.

PARA_A09: Screen and print are different render contexts. A card is ready only when its complete content fits in both, including the footer and the visual space needed by the last line.

## 最后核对 / Verify the result

PARA_A10: Searchable PDF text lets a reader find this sentence later. A picture of a page may look convincing, but it must not silently replace the text in an export that promises native text.

PARA_A11: 对照原稿逐项检查。Every original paragraph should appear once, in order; every link should still point to its original destination; repeated table headers are the deliberate exception elsewhere.

PARA_A12: Finish with a small, reproducible check. Export the same source again in a new directory, inspect the card count, and compare source coverage before judging decorative details.
