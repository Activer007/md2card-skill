## Original code-native art (M7)

Eight additional PNGs are drawn from blank canvases by scripts/create-template-art.py: an explicit fabric-edge detail, three fictional comic scenes, two cucumber-preparation sketches, an open tray, and a lidded box. No outside artwork or photograph is read, edited or cropped. Captions explain their schematic/fictional character; the recipe is an untested demonstration, not measured nutrition or a validated recipe. Product illustrations carry no real brand, price, rating or performance claim.

Reproduce into an absent or empty directory: python scripts/create-template-art.py --out /tmp/md2card-original-art. This optional authoring helper requires Pillow and a local DejaVu Sans font for the two numeric detail labels; font software is not redistributed. It never overwrites a nonempty directory. All eight generated PNGs were reproduced byte-identically in the recorded local environment. Hashes/dimensions are in provenance.json.
